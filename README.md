## Why Sirat? (The Vision)

Most modern Islamic apps suffer from intrusive full-screen advertisements, cluttered menus, paywalled widgets, and heavy battery drain. **Sirat** was built from the ground up to be the exact opposite:
* **100% Free & Ad-Free Forever:** Built purely as *Sadaqah Jariyah* (continuous charity).
* **Architectural Minimalist UI:** Crafted with a deep Matte Pine-Obsidian (`#060D0B`) and Champagne Gold (`#D6B36A`) design language—zero childish emojis, pure vector iconography, and editorial serif typography.
* **True Native Android Integration:** Direct `AppWidgetManager` home screen synchronization and `AlarmManager` exact prayer notifications.

## Comprehensive Feature Breakdown

### 1. First-Launch Interactive Flashcard Onboarding
* Welcomes new users with a smooth **4-Slide Flashcard Deck** explaining every instrument inside the app.
* Includes instant **Profile Name** and **City/Village Setup** on the final flashcard so timings and widgets are personalized from the very first second.
* Can be replayed anytime from the Settings menu.

### 2. Dual-Pane Salah Horizon & Custom Masjid Time Override
* **Astronomical Precision:** Automatically fetches live daily timings (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib/Iftar, Isha) and the Hijri calendar for any city, town, or village worldwide.
* **Fiqh Flexibility:** 1-tap toggle between **Shafi'i** and **Hanafi** Asr shadow calculations, plus **12-Hour (AM/PM)** and **24-Hour** clock formats.
* **Local Masjid Time Override:** Long-press any prayer on the home strip to manually lock your local Masjid's exact Jamaat time.

### 3. Exact Native Prayer Notifications (`AlarmManager`)
* Automatically schedules daily high-priority Android notifications for all 5 obligatory prayers.
* At the exact minute set for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, or Isha, your phone alerts you:
  > **"Sirat • Time for Fajr Prayer"**
  > *"It is time for Fajr. May Allah accept your prayers."*
* Includes a **1-Tap Test Notification** button inside the Widget Studio.

### 4. Direct-Sync Native Home Screen Widgets (2 Types)
* **Widget 1 — Dual-Pane Prayer & Live Chronometer Widget:** Displays your location, username badge, upcoming prayer, all 5 prayer times, and a **real-time ticking Android `Chronometer` countdown** right on your phone wallpaper.
* **Widget 2 — Daily Quranic Manuscript Widget:** Displays the non-repeating Daily Quranic Verse in authentic Arabic script alongside its translation.
* **Full Customization Studio:** Adjust the **Arabic Ayah Font Size (`16sp` – `30sp`)** via a live slider and switch between **3 Luxury Widget Themes** (*Pine Emerald*, *Obsidian Gold*, *Midnight Sapphire*).

### 5. Complete Noble Quran (114 Surahs) in Quran.com Script
* Powered by the official **Uthmani / Amiri Quran Calligraphic Font (`QuranFont.ttf`)** for authentic script rendering.
* Multi-language translation support (**English, Hindi, Urdu, and Hinglish**).
* High-definition **Verse-by-Verse Audio Recitation** by *Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy*.

### 6. Global 10-Book Hadith Vault (36,000+ Hadiths)
* Complete access to the 10 major Sunni Hadith collections:
  1. **Sahih al-Bukhari** (7,589 Hadiths • 97 Chapters)
  2. **Sahih Muslim** (7,563 Hadiths • 56 Chapters)
  3. **Sunan an-Nasa'i** (5,765 Hadiths • 51 Chapters)
  4. **Sunan Abu Dawud** (5,274 Hadiths • 43 Chapters)
  5. **Jami' at-Tirmidhi** (3,998 Hadiths • 49 Chapters)
  6. **Sunan Ibn Majah** (4,343 Hadiths • 37 Chapters)
  7. **Muwatta Imam Malik** (1,858 Hadiths • 61 Chapters)
  8. **An-Nawawi's 40 Hadith** (42 Hadiths)
  9. **Hadith Qudsi** (40 Sacred Hadiths)
  10. **Shah Waliullah's 40 Hadith** (40 Hadiths)
* **Instant Top Global Search:** Search any keyword or Hadith number directly above the books—view clean 2-line preview cards and tap any card to open the full Hadith in a scrollable reader sheet.

### 7. Non-Repeating Daily Ayah & Dua Engine
* Connected live to the **Al-Quran Cloud API** (6,236 verses), deterministically calculating a unique, non-repeating **Verse of the Day** and **Quranic Dua of the Day** for every single day of the year.

### 8. Executive Spiritual Streak & Analytics
* **Radial Consistency Gauge:** Real-time monthly completion percentage and active day streak counter.
* **7-Day Weekly Bar Chart:** Visual breakdown of your last 7 days of Salah performance.
* **Prayer-by-Prayer Discipline Meters:** Individual progress bars for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha to help identify and strengthen your weakest prayer.
* **30-Day Monthly Consistency Matrix:** Clean architectural heatmap of the entire month.

### 9. Owner Admin Control Panel (PIN Protected)
* Built-in **Owner Admin Dashboard** (unlockable via long-press on the Sirat emblem in Settings, Default PIN: `7860`).
* Control live home announcement banners, startup broadcast popups, OTA APK update alerts, custom Ayah/Dua overrides, and developer profile branding without rebuilding the app.

## Technical Architecture

| Layer | Technology / Service | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UI Framework** | Flutter 3.x (Material 3 Dark/Light) | 60fps cross-screen rendering & custom vector painters |
| **Native Android** | Kotlin `MethodChannel` (`com.noor/widgets`) | Direct `AppWidgetManager` force-sync & `AlarmManager` |
| **Calligraphy** | KFGQPC / Amiri Quran TTF (`QuranFont`) | Authentic Quran.com Arabic script rendering |
| **Salah Engine** | Aladhan Astronomical API + Nominatim | Coordinates-based timings & reverse geocoding |
| **Scripture CDN** | Al-Quran Cloud + FawazAhmed Hadith CDN | 114 Surahs audio/text & 36,000+ indexed Hadiths |
| **CI/CD Pipeline** | GitHub Actions (`ubuntu-latest`, Java 17) | Automated cloud APK compilation on every commit |
